Methodology. Prices are the midpoint of each listing's price guide as shown on realestate.com.au (18–21 Sept 2026); a single figure means it was quoted as a fixed price. “Era” tags older-build candidates by proxy signals only — small block size, low unit numbers, modest floor areas — not a confirmed construction year; portals don't reliably state build year. Several suburbs' current listings skew toward newer mid-rise stock rather than genuine six-packs — those are honestly tagged “Newer build (non-target)” rather than mislabelled as older stock. Treat every era tag as an informed guess, not a fact, and check each listing's own description or a title search for the real figure.

Estimated rent is a rough weekly figure from general knowledge of each suburb's rental band for that dwelling type and bedroom count — not a rent appraisal. Estimated yield is simply (rent × 52) ÷ price, the same guide-price-style upper-bound calculation used across this project's UK auction reviews: treat it as a starting point for comparison, not a return you're likely to actually clear once agent fees, vacancy, land tax, owners corporation fees and maintenance are counted.

Area safety reflects general reputation and this researcher's background knowledge of each suburb, not official crime data. For verified, LGA-level figures, check the Crime Statistics Agency Victoria at crimestatistics.vic.gov.au before treating any suburb as safer or riskier than another. The suburb map is a hand-placed schematic for orientation only, not a geocoded or surveyed map — distances and bearings are approximate.

Desk-based screening only — not a valuation, rental appraisal or investment advice. Verify every figure independently (agent, building/strata search, school zone confirmation) before acting on anything here.
