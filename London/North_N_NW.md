# North London (N / NW) — McHugh & Co Auction, 16–17 September 2026
## Batch A: 18 residential lots — area-by-area investment research

Source catalogue: https://www.mchughandco.com/future-auctions/76247 (lot numbers, addresses, guide prices, descriptions, end-times per supplied CSV). Guide prices are auction *opening estimates* only — final hammer prices at UK property auctions commonly land 10–40% above guide, especially for vacant-possession lots in strong-demand areas. Treat "guide vs local average" comparisons below as a discount *signal*, not a guaranteed bargain — condition, lease terms, and competitive bidding on the day all move the outcome.

Detail-page hunting: only **1 of 18** lots had a findable, verifiable dedicated listing page on mchughandco.com (Lot 84, Bowes Road) — consistent with the heads-up that this catalogue only went live shortly before this research and isn't yet well indexed. All other lots below are researched from the catalogue-level CSV data (address, guide, description) plus independent area research (Rightmove sold-price data via HM Land Registry, general market/regeneration/schools knowledge). This is noted per property.

Area price data source: Rightmove "House Prices" postcode-district pages (HM Land Registry sold-price data, trailing 12 months, retrieved live).

## 2. Hampstead Village (NW3)

**Rating**
- Growth potential: **4/5** — Ultra-prime London has cooled from its 2022 peak (NW3 overall −10%) but Hampstead Village retains structurally constrained supply (conservation area, listed buildings) that supports long-run appreciation once the cycle turns.
- Average comparable cost: NW3-wide average terraced £2,461,544 (this figure is skewed upward by large detached/semi mansions across the whole postcode). A small period cottage on Holly Mount — one of the prettiest, most tightly-held mews in Hampstead Village — would realistically fetch well over £1m even modestly sized; a £500,000+ guide looks very low and likely reflects a compact footprint and/or refurbishment need. Strong potential discount signal, but verify size/condition before assuming a bargain.

- Proximity to shops/amenities: **5/5** — Hampstead High Street, Heath, boutiques and restaurants on the doorstep.
- Proximity to schools: **5/5** — South Hampstead High School, University College School (UCS), and well-regarded state primaries all close by.
- **Verdict:** Small guide relative to Hampstead Village norms — likely the single most interesting value story in the batch if the cottage's size/condition stack up.
