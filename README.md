# McHugh & Co Auction — 16-17 September 2026 — Property Review

Source: McHugh & Co's online auction catalogue (https://www.mchughandco.com/future-auctions/76247), 323 numbered lots (~365 lots including sub-lettered ones). Forwarded from lequang@gmail.com on 28 Aug 2026.

**Scope:** filtered the full catalogue down to residential lots (houses/flats — garages, land-only, commercial units, office buildings, and ground-rent-only lots excluded) located in **Manchester, Birmingham, and London**. Result: **65 properties** — 59 London, 4 Manchester, 2 Birmingham — across roughly 43 distinct neighborhoods.

Each property was checked against its individual McHugh & Co listing page where findable (most were not yet search-indexed, since the catalogue only went live 27 Aug 2026 — those use the catalogue's own summary data: address, guide price, property type, vacant possession status). Every neighborhood got its own research pass: recent/forecast price growth, how the guide price compares to typical local sale prices for a similar property, social status/desirability, and proximity to shops and schools.

## Method notes / limitations

- Direct access to mchughandco.com is blocked by bot protection for automated fetching; a public reader proxy (r.jina.ai) was used to get past this where individual listing pages could be located at all.
- The auction catalogue went live only 27 Aug 2026, so almost none of the 65 lots have an individually search-indexed detail page yet — the large majority of entries in each file are built from the catalogue's own summary (address, guide price, property type/description) plus independent area research, not each property's full listing page. This is noted per-lot in each file.
- Guide prices are sellers' minimum expectations, not predicted sale prices, and can change up to the day of auction — treat all "discount vs. average" figures as indicative, not final.
- All 5 batches were researched independently in parallel, so cross-batch comparisons (e.g. the "top picks" list above) are informal — no single consistent scoring formula was applied across all 65 lots.
