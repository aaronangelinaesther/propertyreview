# UK Auction Property Reviews

Investment-screening reviews of UK residential property auction catalogues. Each review filters a full catalogue down to residential lots (houses / flats — garages, land-only, ground-rent-only and commercial lots excluded) in **Greater London, Greater Manchester, and the City of Birmingham**, then gives every lot:

- an **area rating** — growth potential, guide price vs comparable local sale prices, social status / desirability, proximity to shops, proximity to schools;
- a **rental estimate and gross yield** — annual rent ÷ guide price, plus a fair-value yield against the researched local comparable;
- **per-lot risk flags** — short leases, ex-local-authority / housing-association tenure, regulated tenancies, cladding / service-charge exposure, HMO-consent risk, condition.

## At a glance

### McHugh & Co — 16–17 September 2026
Smaller-lot, refurbishment-heavy catalogue skewed hard to London (mostly sub-£300k leasehold flats and freehold terraces needing modernisation). Individual lot detail pages were eventually located for 47 of 66 lots, so many lots have **auctioneer-confirmed lease length, EPC and accommodation**. Headline picks: the Tottenham (N17) freehold four-flat blocks with confirmed live tenancies (~6.6–6.8% verified), the Ruislip / Mottingham value plays, and the Manchester/Birmingham lots which all clear ~6.8%+ on fair value. Recurring risk: leasehold flats priced at 5–15% of comparable value (short-lease / age-restricted anomalies — Chelsea, Balham, Bromley).

### Savills — 15 & 16 September 2026
Larger, broader UK-wide sale (188 residential lots + a commercial section 201–300, excluded). The catalogue index fetched cleanly but individual lot detail pages did not via the research tools — they *are* viewable through a signed-in Savills account, and **~16 lots (all the recommended ones plus several flagged) have since been verified that way**, correcting a number of first-pass calls (e.g. Lot 45's rent is £50,700 with a regulated tenancy, not ~£65k; Lots 96/97 have fresh 125-yr leases, not short ones; Lot 40 is a basement flat). Headline picks: Leyton Lot 7 (freehold house, two vacant flats), **Deansgate Square Lot 93 (confirmed £36,000/yr let → ~11.5% gross on guide)**, Hayes Lot 37 (confirmed freehold 4-bed, Crossrail), Forest Hill Lot 8 (confirmed fresh 181-yr lease, good order, at half local value). Recurring risk: ~12 leasehold flats still guiding at 35–55% of local value with tenure unconfirmed (short lease / ex-social-landlord), plus one £1.4m Balham building whose ~9–10% HMO case hinges on a 13-room large-HMO planning consent.

## Shared method & limitations

- **Gross yield is calculated against the guide price**, which is a seller's minimum — auction lots typically sell above guide, so every guide-price yield is an upper bound. A second fair-value yield is given against each area's researched comparable sale price.
- **Regional pattern (consistent across both reviews):** Manchester/Birmingham lots clear ~6–9% gross on fair value; London lots only reach that on discounted guides and otherwise sit in the 4.5–6% band — high London "yields" in these catalogues usually flag a pricing/tenure anomaly rather than a strong rental market.
- Area and comparable-price research is from web search plus Zoopla / Rightmove / OnTheMarket / Land Registry postcode price pages and council/developer regeneration pages. No single consistent scoring formula was applied across all lots; batches were researched independently, so cross-batch comparisons are informal judgement calls.
- These are desk-based screening notes, **not** valuations or advice — every shortlisted lot still needs the legal pack, a survey and independent local pricing before bidding.
