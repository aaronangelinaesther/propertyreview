## Risk flags worth checking before bidding on anything

- **"Too good to be true" yields (Chelsea 44.8%, Balham 36.0%)** — almost certainly short-lease pricing anomalies. Do not take at face value.
- **Leasehold flats priced at 5-15% of comparable value** (South batch: Lots 46, 48, 77, 136, 161) — near-certainly a short lease or age-restricted tenure, not a genuine bargain. Verify via the legal pack.
- **Lot 63, Ruislip** — now confirmed: only ~12 years left on the lease. This explains the steep "discount" — a mortgage/re-sale on a lease this short is difficult.
- **Lot 7, Edgware (West batch)** — large apparent discount traced to a documented subsidence history.
- **Lots 65/81, Hounslow (West batch)** — large apparent discount traced to likely non-standard construction.
- **Lot 156, Erdington (Manchester/Birmingham batch)** — decent discount but flagged for higher local crime.
- **Lot 4, Chingford E4 (East batch)** — largest apparent discount in that batch, but the E4 "semi-detached average" comparable may be based on a thin sample — worth independently verifying before trusting the gap.

## Method notes / limitations

- Direct access to mchughandco.com is blocked by bot protection for automated fetching; the `r.jina.ai` reader proxy was used throughout — both for fetching known listing URLs and, once native search quota ran out, for routing DuckDuckGo/Bing searches to find them.
- 47 of 66 lots now have a confirmed current-auction detail page; the remaining 19 (mostly in the South London batch) are still built from the catalogue's own summary (address, guide price, property type/description) plus independent area research. This is noted per-lot in each file.
- Rental estimates are gross yields against the **guide price**, not the likely hammer price — guide prices are sellers' minimum expectations and auction lots typically sell above guide, so treat every yield figure here as an upper bound, not a promise.
- Where a rental-focused property site could not be reached directly (Zoopla/Rightmove/Home.co.uk/PropertyData all blocked automated access in some sessions), rent estimates fall back to postcode/property-type knowledge and comparable area benchmarks rather than a live listings pull — flagged per-file where this applies. Where an actual confirmed passing rent was found on a tenanted lot's own listing page, that figure is used instead and called out explicitly.
- All 5 batches were researched independently in parallel, so cross-batch comparisons (e.g. the "top picks" lists above) are informal — no single consistent scoring formula was applied across all 66 lots.
