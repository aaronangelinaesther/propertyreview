# Savills Auction — 29 & 30 September 2026 (Catalogue #243) — London West Batch

Catalogue: https://auctions.savills.co.uk/auctions/29--30-september-2026-243 — UK-wide sale. This batch covers the **21 West London residential lots** in scope: Harrow, Hammersmith & Fulham, Kensington & Chelsea, Westminster, Ealing, Hillingdon, Hounslow, Richmond (Barnes) and Brent(west)/Acton postcodes. Unless noted, all lots are **"To be offered on Tuesday 29 September 2026"**, remote bidding only. Guide prices are seller minimums — auction lots typically sell above guide, so treat the guide-price yield as an upper bound; a second **fair-value** yield is given against each area's researched local comparable. Ratings 1 (weak) – 5 (strong).

**Method:** built from the catalogue lot descriptions supplied plus independent research (Zoopla / Rightmove / OnTheMarket sold-price and rent pages, council/developer regeneration pages, Ofsted, web search for rental comparables). No Savills-account detail-page access was available this pass, so accommodation/lease/EPC detail is limited to what the catalogue text itself states — flagged "Catalogue text only" throughout except Lot 20, which carries confirmed detail inherited from the previous catalogue's identical listing (see below).
