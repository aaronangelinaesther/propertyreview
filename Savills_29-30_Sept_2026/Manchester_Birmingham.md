**Rental method:** gross yield is annual rent ÷ **guide price**, plus a second **fair-value** yield against a researched local comparable price. Guide prices are sellers' minimums and auction lots typically sell above guide, so treat the guide-price yield as an upper bound. Where the catalogue confirms a passing rent ("Investment let at £X per annum"), that figure is used as CONFIRMED rather than re-estimated. Ratings use a 1 (weak) – 5 (strong) scale. Research date: 17 September 2026 (auction is 29–30 September 2026); "current" listings/news reflect that date.

**Research note:** individual Savills lot detail pages were not reachable this pass (no signed-in account access this session), so all eight lots rely on the catalogue index text plus independent research — Zoopla/Rightmove/OnTheMarket sold-price and rental pages, and general web search. Two lots (13, 340) sit in thin data segments — a retirement/sheltered scheme in Farnworth and a small Daubhill flat market — where comparable sales and rental evidence were sparse; this is flagged in each section.
