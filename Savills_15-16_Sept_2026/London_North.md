# Savills Auction — 15 & 16 September 2026 (Catalogue #242) — London North (N / NW) Batch

Catalogue: https://auctions.savills.co.uk/auctions/15--16-september-2026-242
Batch scope: North London residential lots — N and NW postcode areas. **14 lots across 12 neighbourhoods.**

**Site accessibility:** the Savills catalogue index pages fetch cleanly; individual Savills lot detail pages were not reliably reachable this pass. Each lot table below is built from the catalogue index (lot number, address, type, guide, tenure, vacant/tenanted) plus independent area research — HM Land Registry / Zoopla / Rightmove sold-price and rent pages, council regeneration pages, and web search for local rental comparables.

**Method:** gross yield = annual rent ÷ **guide price** (an upper bound — auction lots typically sell above guide), with a second **fair-value** yield against the researched local comparable price. Ratings: 1 (weak) – 5 (strong).

**Cross-cutting flag for this batch:** several leasehold flats here guide at **35–60% of the normal local flat value** (Lots 29, 49, 58, 90, 96, 100, 130). Gaps that large are almost always a **short unexpired lease** (or, less often, ex-local-authority tenure / a very small unit), not simple underpricing. The legal pack's lease length, ground rent and service charge must be checked before any yield figure on those lots is treated as real. Lot 101 (a *freehold house* at ~50% of value) is a different but related red flag — that pattern usually means a **protected/regulated tenant in situ** with a sub-market passing rent and no vacant possession.
