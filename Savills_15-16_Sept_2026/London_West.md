# Savills Auction — 15 & 16 September 2026 (Catalogue #242) — London West (W / HA / UB / TW) Batch

Catalogue: https://auctions.savills.co.uk/auctions/15--16-september-2026-242
Batch scope: West / North-West London residential lots — W, plus outer-west HA (Harrow / Wembley), UB (Hayes) and TW (Brentford) postcodes. **18 lots across 13 neighbourhoods.**

**Site accessibility / method:** built from the Savills catalogue index plus independent area research (Land Registry / Zoopla / Rightmove sold-price and rent pages, council/developer regeneration pages, web search for rental comparables). **Lots 37, 39, 40 and 61 have since been verified against their Savills detail pages** (signed-in account, 4 Sept 2026), and the fuller catalogue text was re-read for Lots 77, 81, 82 and 97 — corrections are flagged in each section. Gross yield = annual rent ÷ **guide price** (upper bound); a second **fair-value** yield is given against the local comparable. Ratings 1 (weak) – 5 (strong).

**Cross-cutting notes for this batch:**
- **Lots on genuinely clean, long tenure:** Lot 40 (new 125-yr lease, peppercorn GR — *but a lower-ground/basement flat*), Lot 41 (new 125-yr lease), Lots 81, 82 **and 97** (new 125-yr leases, peppercorn GR — Lot 97 was previously mis-flagged as short-lease), Lot 77 (~108-yr lease), Lot 98-equivalent stock. Where the guide is still well below local value on these, the discount is *not* a lease problem — look at unit size, floor/aspect, or position (basement, above-shops, main-road).
- **Lot 39 (Acton) is share of freehold *but on a 74-year underlying lease*** — extendable cheaply via the share of freehold, provided the freehold company is properly set up and the co-owner(s) co-operate. Verify that structure.
- **Genuinely unconfirmed lease risk remains on Lots 22 & 106 (Paddington mansion flats)** — no lease term disclosed; the classic W2 mansion-block short-lease pattern. Price the statutory extension before bidding.
- **Ex-estate / ex-local-authority stock:** Lot 38 (Cleverly Estate, W12) and Lot 34 (Alexandra Court, Wembley) — check service charges, major-works liabilities and any resale restriction.
- **Lot 61 (Ealing)** is an age-restricted retirement flat on a ~62-year lease, **confirmed let at £18,000/yr** — strong running yield, but niche, illiquid and exposed to exit/"event" fees and heavy service charges.
- **The Elizabeth Line runs through much of this batch** — Hanwell (16), Acton (39), Hayes (37), Ealing (61), Paddington (22/106/107) all have a Crossrail station, a real and delivered demand support.
