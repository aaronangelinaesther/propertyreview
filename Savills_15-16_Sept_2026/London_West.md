# Savills Auction — 15 & 16 September 2026 (Catalogue #242) — London West (W / HA / UB / TW) Batch

Catalogue: https://auctions.savills.co.uk/auctions/15--16-september-2026-242
Batch scope: West / North-West London residential lots — W, plus outer-west HA (Harrow / Wembley), UB (Hayes) and TW (Brentford) postcodes. **18 lots across 13 neighbourhoods.**

**Site accessibility / method:** built from the Savills catalogue index plus independent area research (Land Registry / Zoopla / Rightmove sold-price and rent pages, council/developer regeneration pages, web search for rental comparables). Individual Savills lot detail pages were not reliably reachable this pass. Gross yield = annual rent ÷ **guide price** (upper bound); a second **fair-value** yield is given against the local comparable. Ratings 1 (weak) – 5 (strong).

**Cross-cutting notes for this batch:**
- **Four lots come with a clean, explicitly-stated tenure** — Lot 40 (new 125-year lease), Lot 41 (new 125-year lease), Lots 81 & 82 (new 125-year leases, peppercorn ground rent), plus Lot 39 (share of freehold). Where the guide is still well below local value on those, the discount is *not* a lease problem — look instead at unit size, floor/aspect, or position.
- **Prime mansion-block leaseholds at ~50% of value** (Lots 22, 97, and to an extent 106) — the classic short-lease signature in W2 / W11 stucco and mansion-block stock. Price the statutory extension before bidding.
- **Ex-estate / ex-local-authority stock:** Lot 38 (Cleverly Estate, W12) and Lot 34 (Alexandra Court, Wembley) — check service charges, major-works liabilities and any resale restriction.
- **Lot 61 (Ealing) is an age-restricted retirement flat on a 62-year lease** — a niche, illiquid sub-market where headline yields are unreliable.
- **The Elizabeth Line runs through much of this batch** — Hanwell (16), Acton (39), Hayes (37), Ealing (61), Paddington (22/106/107) all have a Crossrail station, a real and delivered demand support.
