# Savills Auction — 15 & 16 September 2026 (Catalogue #242) — London South (SE / SW / CR / SM) Batch

Catalogue: https://auctions.savills.co.uk/auctions/15--16-september-2026-242
Batch scope: South London residential lots — SE, SW, CR and SM (Sutton) postcode areas. **18 lots across 16 neighbourhoods.**

**Site accessibility / method:** built from the Savills catalogue index plus independent area research (Land Registry / Zoopla / Rightmove sold-price and rent pages, council regeneration pages, web search for rental comparables). Individual Savills lot detail pages were not reliably reachable this pass. Gross yield = annual rent ÷ **guide price** (upper bound); a second **fair-value** yield is given against the local comparable. Ratings 1 (weak) – 5 (strong).

**Cross-cutting flags for this batch:**
- **Guides at 35–50% of local value** (Lots 11, 46, 69, 73, 89, 119, 162) — near-always a short lease, ex-local-authority / ex-housing-association (right-to-buy) tenure, or a very small unit. Three lots (69 Peabody Hill, 89 Peabody Estate Rodney Road, and to a lesser extent 69's neighbours) are explicitly on **Peabody estates** — check for resale nomination restrictions, heavy service charges and pending major-works bills.
- **Two lots have no published guide** — Lot 95 (Stockwell) and, in effect, nothing else here — Lot 95 cannot be assessed until the guide appears.
- **Lot 12 (Balham, £1.4m)** is the single most expensive lot in the whole review — a semi-detached HMO on the A24; its value case depends entirely on the HMO being lawfully established and licensable under Wandsworth's Article 4 regime.
- **Lot 113 (Merton Park) is sold in "shell condition"** — budget a full fit-out (~£40,000–£60,000) on top of the guide.
