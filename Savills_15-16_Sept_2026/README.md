Source: Savills online auction catalogue — https://auctions.savills.co.uk/auctions/15--16-september-2026-242 (link forwarded 3 Sept 2026). A UK-wide sale: **Lots 1–188 residential** (main session Tuesday 15 September, 9:00am, remote bidding only), plus a **commercial section, Lots 201–300, on 16 September**. *Savills' own text on Lot 7 gives the date as both "Tuesday 14 September" and 15 September — confirm any specific lot's date directly.*

**Scope:** the same filter as the [McHugh & Co review](../McHugh_16-17_Sept_2026/README.md) — residential lots (houses / flats; garages, land-only, ground-rent-only and commercial lots excluded) in **Greater Manchester, City of Birmingham, and Greater London**. Every lot has: an area rating (growth potential, guide-vs-comparable value, social status/desirability, proximity to shops, proximity to schools), a **rental estimate and gross yield** (annual rent ÷ guide price, plus a fair-value yield against the researched local comparable), and per-lot risk flags.

## Method notes / limitations

- The Savills catalogue index fetched without obstruction; individual lot detail pages did not, on the first pass. On 4 Sept 2026 **~16 lots were verified against their detail pages via the user's signed-in Savills account** (listed under Site accessibility) — those carry confirmed lease / EPC / accommodation / passing rent, and several first-pass flags were corrected as a result. The remaining ~55 lots' tenure/lease notes are still **inferences from the guide-to-comparable gap**, not legal-pack facts — a step down in certainty from the McHugh review (which eventually located 47 of 66 detail pages).
- Rental estimates are **gross yields against the guide price** (a seller's minimum — auction lots typically sell above guide, so treat every guide-price yield as an upper bound), alongside a **fair-value yield** against each area's researched comparable sale price.
- Area and comparable-price research is from web search plus Zoopla / Rightmove / OnTheMarket / Land Registry postcode price pages and council/developer regeneration pages, current to early September 2026. Where a portal's rent statistics were not directly fetchable, rent figures fall back to postcode/property-type benchmarks and comparable listings.
- The five batches were researched sequentially, not in parallel, and **no single consistent scoring formula was applied across all 73 lots** — cross-batch "top picks" are informal judgement calls.
- **Explicitly excluded** from scope: the commercial section (Lots 201–300); garage blocks and land (Lots 52, 62, 76, 186); ground-rent investments (Lot 122); and residential lots outside Greater London / Greater Manchester / City of Birmingham — e.g. Chigwell IG7 (Essex), Slough (Berks), Epsom (Surrey), Hoddesdon (Herts), and Walsall / other Black Country (West Midlands but not Birmingham).
