# Savills Auction — 15 & 16 September 2026 (Catalogue #242) — Property Review

Source: Savills online auction catalogue — https://auctions.savills.co.uk/auctions/15--16-september-2026-242 (link forwarded 3 Sept 2026). A UK-wide sale: **Lots 1–188 residential** (auctioned across 14–16 September; a small number of lots such as Lot 7 are held on **Monday 14 September**, a day ahead of the main sale), plus a **commercial section, Lots 201–300, on 16 September**.

**Scope:** the same filter as the McHugh & Co review in the repo root — residential lots (houses / flats; garages, land-only, ground-rent-only and commercial lots excluded) in **Greater Manchester, City of Birmingham, and Greater London**. Result: **73 lots** — 66 London, 6 Greater Manchester, 1 Birmingham — across roughly 50 neighbourhoods.

Every lot has: an area rating (growth potential, guide-vs-comparable value, social status/desirability, proximity to shops, proximity to schools), a **rental estimate and gross yield** (annual rent ÷ guide price, plus a fair-value yield against the researched local comparable), and per-lot risk flags.

## Site accessibility

- The Savills **catalogue index pages fetch cleanly** for automated research (no proxy needed — unlike mchughandco.com in the earlier review).
- **Individual Savills lot detail pages were *not* reliably reachable this pass.** This is the single biggest limitation of this review versus the McHugh one: there is **no per-lot confirmation of lease length, EPC, council tax band, or full accommodation** from the auctioneer's own pages. Each lot table is built from the catalogue index (lot number, address, property type, guide price, tenure, vacant/tenanted) plus independent area research.
- **Consequence:** every "short lease likely" / "ex-local-authority likely" flag in the batch files is **inferred from the size of the guide-to-comparable-value gap**, not confirmed from a legal pack. Treat those as "read the legal pack here first", not as established fact.

**Regional pattern (confirmed, and consistent with the earlier review):** every Greater Manchester / Birmingham lot clears ~6% gross on fair value and the northern terraces clear ~7–8.5%, in line with the 6–9% range commonly cited for Manchester/Birmingham buy-to-let. London lots only reach those levels **on the guide price**, where the guide is heavily discounted — and in this catalogue the discount is very often a short lease, ex-social-landlord tenure, or a cladding/condition problem. On a *fair-value* basis London lots land in the usual **4.5–6%** band.

Verify lease length, ground rent and service charge before trusting any yield.

## Method notes / limitations

- The Savills catalogue index fetched without obstruction; **individual lot detail pages did not**, so this review has **no auctioneer-confirmed lease lengths, EPCs, council tax bands or room-by-room accommodation** — a material step down in per-lot certainty from the McHugh review (which eventually located 47 of 66 detail pages). All tenure/lease inferences here come from the guide-to-comparable gap.
- Rental estimates are **gross yields against the guide price** (a seller's minimum — auction lots typically sell above guide, so treat every guide-price yield as an upper bound), alongside a **fair-value yield** against each area's researched comparable sale price.
- Area and comparable-price research is from web search plus Zoopla / Rightmove / OnTheMarket / Land Registry postcode price pages and council/developer regeneration pages, current to early September 2026. Where a portal's rent statistics were not directly fetchable, rent figures fall back to postcode/property-type benchmarks and comparable listings.
- The five batches were researched sequentially, not in parallel, and **no single consistent scoring formula was applied across all 73 lots** — cross-batch "top picks" are informal judgement calls.
- **Explicitly excluded** from scope: the commercial section (Lots 201–300); garage blocks and land (Lots 52, 62, 76, 186); ground-rent investments (Lot 122); and residential lots outside Greater London / Greater Manchester / City of Birmingham — e.g. Chigwell IG7 (Essex), Slough (Berks), Epsom (Surrey), Hoddesdon (Herts), and Walsall / other Black Country (West Midlands but not Birmingham).
